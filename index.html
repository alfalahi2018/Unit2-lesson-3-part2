Teacher plan · 25 minutes

Class: Grade 7 · Unit 2: Awesome animals · Lesson 3, part 2 (Listening and speaking, audio 2.6 and 2.7)
Lesson aim: to use modals for deduction (may, might, could, can’t, must) in listening and speaking.
Materials: Class Book audio 2.6 and 2.7, pictures from the lesson, notebooks, device or projector, speakers.

TimeStageWhat happensTeacher’s Book link

Channel 3: Cat and Toy		Listen and choose: the farmer’s cat and the toy. Students then read the questions and answer after listening again.Listen and choose (2.6): questions 2 and 3. Listen again and answer the questions (2.7).

20–25Closing

Differentiation

  • Support 🌱: the audio script is shown, two answer choices, and sentence starters.
  • Standard 🌿: script hidden, with a Script button available (fewer points if used).
  • Challenge 🌳: only two plays of each recording, and three sentences in the Radio Report.

Notes and keys

  • Key: eagle · black body and white paws · dolphin.
  • The recordings are read aloud by the browser (text to speech). They are scripts written for this game, based on the topics in the Teacher’s Book. Use your own Class Book audio 2.6 and 2.7 as well, and edit the LQ list in the code if you want the exact scripts.
  • Deaf/hard of hearing students: use the Script button, gestures and written answers.
  • The Script button is also a backup if a device has no voice.
